## 📌 1. Project Overview

How can a student-focused AI workshop acquire 500 verified final-year engineering student registrations in seven days with a marketing budget of just ₹2,000?

The **NxtWave Workshop Growth Strategy & Execution Engine** addresses this challenge through a combination of audience research, peer-driven distribution, referral incentives, registration funnel optimization, and campaign analytics.

Instead of relying exclusively on a static landing page, the project explores an integrated platform connecting student acquisition, referral growth, workshop engagement, and performance measurement.

### 🎯 Core Objectives

- Acquire 500 verified final-year engineering student registrations within seven days.
- Optimize acquisition costs using a ₹2,000 budget.
- Encourage peer-to-peer referrals through milestone-based incentives.
- Improve workshop attendance through structured pre-event communication.
- Track campaign performance using measurable growth metrics.

## 🧠 2. Target Audience & Problem Statement

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)**

- **Audience:** Final-year B.Tech/B.E. students graduating in 2026–2027.
- **Streams:** CSE, IT, AI/ML, Data Science, ECE, and related disciplines.
- **Primary needs:** Placement-ready projects, practical AI skills, stronger GitHub portfolios, and interview preparation.
- **Key barriers:** Limited practical experience, uncertainty about AI development, time constraints, and concerns about project originality.

### Why This Workshop?

The campaign positions the workshop around a practical outcome: helping students understand and build a small AI-powered application in a guided session.

**Value proposition:** Learn by building, explore modern AI tools, and leave with a practical foundation for a portfolio project.

### Seven-Day Execution Roadmap

1. **Day 1:** Recruit initial college representatives and test the registration funnel.
2. **Day 2:** Distribute workshop information through relevant student communities.
3. **Day 3:** Activate referral milestones and measure referral participation.
4. **Day 4:** Publish project-focused LinkedIn content and contact technical clubs.
5. **Day 5:** Expand outreach through college representatives and community partners.
6. **Day 6:** Send accurate event reminders and address registration drop-offs.
7. **Day 7:** Send final reminders, confirm registrations, and prepare the workshop.

## 💰 5. Campaign Economics

The campaign uses the following planning calculations:

**Projected cost per registration**

₹2,000 ÷ 520 projected registrations = **₹3.85 per registration**, rounded to two decimal places.

**Budget utilization**

- College representative incentives: ₹1,500.
- Technical community incentive: ₹500.
- Total planned budget: ₹2,000.

**Attendance planning**

At a 70% attendance rate across 520 registrations, the model projects 364 attendees.

These are scenario calculations, not guarantees. The final report should compare actual spending, verified registrations, and attendance against the projections.

## 📊 6. Measurement Framework

The campaign should be evaluated using operational metrics rather than registration volume alone.

| Metric | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| Verified Registrations | Unique, eligible student signups |
| Cost per Verified Registration | Actual campaign spend ÷ verified registrations |
| Referral Conversion Rate | Referred visitors who complete registration |
| Channel Conversion Rate | Registrations attributed to each acquisition channel |
| Attendance Rate | Attendees ÷ verified registrations |
| Budget Utilization | Actual spend compared with the ₹2,000 budget |
